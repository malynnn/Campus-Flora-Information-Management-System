The University is dedicated to ensuring the conservation, restoration, and sustainable use of the campus ecosystem. By highlighting the need for increased awareness and appreciation of urban biodiversity, efforts to research campus flora and fauna enables the formation of measures to protect local species, promote environmental stewardship, and serve as a living botanical database for the PUPQC community.
