Campus Flora Information Management System

The University is dedicated to ensuring the conservation, restoration, and sustainable use of the campus ecosystem. By highlighting the need for increased awareness and appreciation of urban biodiversity, efforts to research campus flora and fauna enables the formation of measures to protect local species, promote environmental stewardship, and serve as a living botanical database for the PUPQC community.

Research

Scientific documentation and botanical survey of plant species within the Polytechnic University of the Philippines — Quezon City (PUPQC) campus grounds.

Objectives
  • Document and catalog all plant species found within the PUPQC campus grounds.
  • Identify native, endemic, and introduced plant species.
  • Assess the ecological roles and medicinal significance of campus flora.
  • Create a living digital botanical database accessible to the PUPQC community.
  • Support biodiversity conservation and environmental stewardship programs.
Methodology
  • Field Survey: Systematic campus-wide transect walks and plot-based sampling within PUPQC grounds.
  • Species Identification: Morphological characterization using botanical keys and PUP Herbarium references.
  • Expert Verification: All identified species were verified by the Plant Biology Students of the Department of Biology, PUP Sta. Mesa in collaboration with the PUP Herbarium.
  • Photography: High-resolution documentation of leaves, bark, flowers, and fruits.
  • GIS Mapping: GPS-tagged location recording for each documented specimen.
  • Database Entry: Structured data encoding into the PUPQC Flora Information System.
